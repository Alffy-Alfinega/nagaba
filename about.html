About NAGABA Association

Founded on 19th August 2024, NAGABA Women Youth and Needy Association is a community-based organisation established by rural people in Kagango, Ntungamo District, Uganda.

Our founding members recognised their societal needs and the inadequacy of individual efforts. By organising collectively, they resolved to further their social and economic cause, earn a stronger position in society, and promote their livelihoods — with a special focus on women and children affected by HIV/AIDS.

Get in Touch

Our Mission

To create well-meaning life situations for rural women and youth through HIV/AIDS awareness, treatment and care, advancing women's rights, and empowering them through skills training and income-generating projects.

Our Vision

A community where every woman, youth, and vulnerable person has the skills, knowledge, and opportunities to lead a dignified, self-sufficient life free from the burden of HIV/AIDS.

Our Values

Community Health

We raise HIV/AIDS awareness within our communities, providing education, support, and care for those infected and affected.

Women's Rights

We advocate for the rights of women and children, championing equality and protection within our communities.

Self-Reliance

We empower members through skills in crafts, tailoring, knitting, business planning, and financial management.

Livelihoods

We create income-generating opportunities through bee keeping, fish farming, cattle rearing, poultry, goat rearing, and cash crops.

Collective Action

We bring together a collective force to influence district planning and ensure social sectors focus on women and youth.

Environmental Protection

We are committed to protecting the environment and promoting sustainable agricultural practices in our communities.

Our Journey

August 2024

The Beginning

Eight rural women recognised their collective strength and founded NAGABA Women Youth and Needy Association in Kagango, Ntungamo District.

August 2024

Constitution Adopted

The constitution was formally adopted on 19th August 2024, establishing our governance structure, membership categories, and organisational objectives.

August 2024

CBO Registration Applied

NAGABA formally applied for registration as a Community Based Organisation with the District Community Development Officer, Rubaare Town Council.

2024–Present

Growing Our Impact

NAGABA is now preparing its first programmes — HIV/AIDS awareness outreaches, skills training workshops, and agricultural projects — to serve communities across Ntungamo District.

Our Partners & Collaborators

We work with NGOs, CBOs, FBOs, and government bodies to care for and support women and children in our community.
