About ChangeMakers

Founded in 2012, ChangeMakers is a non-profit organization dedicated to empowering underserved communities through education, health, and outreach programs.

Our journey began with a small group of passionate individuals who believed in the power of collective action. Today, we serve thousands of families, children, and individuals, helping them build brighter futures.

Get in Touch

Our Mission

To create positive, lasting change by addressing critical needs and fostering hope in every community we serve.

Our Vision

A world where every person has the opportunity to thrive, regardless of their background or circumstances.

Our Values

Compassion

We approach our work with empathy and understanding, recognizing the dignity of every individual we serve.

Integrity

We maintain the highest ethical standards in all our actions, building trust through honesty and accountability.

Collaboration

We believe in the power of partnership and work together with communities, organizations, and individuals.

Empowerment

We strive to build capacity and self-sufficiency, enabling individuals and communities to lead their own development.

Transparency

We are open about our operations, finances, and decision-making processes, ensuring accountability to all stakeholders.

Sustainability

We develop solutions that are environmentally responsible and can be maintained by communities over the long term.

Our Journey

2012

The Beginning

ChangeMakers was founded by a group of five community activists who saw a need for coordinated action in underserved neighborhoods.

2015

Expanding Our Reach

We launched our first major education initiative, providing after-school programs to over 200 children in five communities.

2018

Health Services Begin

Our health outreach program was established, bringing basic healthcare services to remote and underserved areas.

2020

Crisis Response

We rapidly adapted our programs to respond to the global pandemic, providing emergency food assistance and virtual education support.

2023

New Headquarters

We opened our new community center, creating a hub for our programs and a gathering place for the community.

Today

Looking Forward

With a dedicated team and thousands of volunteers, we continue to grow our impact and reach more communities in need.
