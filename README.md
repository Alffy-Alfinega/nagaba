# NAGABA Women Youth and Needy Association

A community-based organisation (CBO) founded on 19th August 2024 in Kagango, Rubaare Town Council, Ntungamo District, Uganda.

## About

NAGABA was established by rural people to collectively address the social and economic needs of women, youth, and the needy — especially those infected and affected by HIV/AIDS — through self-help initiatives.

## Objectives

- Raise HIV/AIDS awareness within the community
- Advocate for the rights of women and children
- Build capacity among NGOs, CBOs, FBOs, and government to support those affected
- Create income-generating opportunities (bee keeping, fish farming, piggery, poultry, goat projects, cash crops)
- Empower through skills training (crafts, tailoring, knitting, business planning, financial management)
- Protect the environment
