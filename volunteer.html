Volunteer With Us

Volunteers are the heart of ChangeMakers. Join our passionate community and help us create lasting change.

Whether you have a few hours or a few months, your time makes a difference! We offer flexible opportunities that match your skills, interests, and schedule.

Apply Now Explore Opportunities

Why Volunteer With Us?

Make an Impact

Your time directly helps those in need. See the immediate difference you make in people's lives through our community-focused programs.

Build Community

Connect with like-minded individuals who share your passion for service. Many of our volunteers form lasting friendships and professional networks.

Gain Skills

Develop valuable skills and experience that enhance your resume. We provide training and mentorship to help you grow personally and professionally.

Current Opportunities

Community Outreach

Distribute food and supplies, connect with families, and support our monthly drives. Help ensure that essential resources reach those who need them most.

  • 4-8 hours per month
  • Various locations
  • Teams of 3-5 people
Apply Now

Event Organizer

Help plan and run fundraising events, health fairs, and educational workshops. Put your organizational and people skills to work creating impactful community events.

  • 6-10 hours per event
  • Office & event venues
  • Committee-based
Apply Now

Education Mentor

Tutor children and adults, lead workshops, and inspire lifelong learning. Share your knowledge and help others develop skills for a brighter future.

  • 2-4 hours per week
  • Schools & learning centers
  • One-on-one or small groups
Apply Now

Health Services Support

Assist at health screenings, vaccination clinics, and wellness workshops. Help community members access vital health information and services.

  • 4-6 hours per event
  • Health fairs & clinics
  • Teams of 5-10 people
Apply Now

Digital Skills Coach

Teach basic computer skills, internet safety, and digital literacy to underserved populations. Help bridge the digital divide in our community.

  • 2-3 hours per week
  • Community centers & libraries
  • Small group instruction
Apply Now

Advocacy & Outreach

Help raise awareness about important community issues, engage with stakeholders, and support our advocacy campaigns for positive change.

  • Flexible hours
  • Various locations & remote
  • Individual & team projects
