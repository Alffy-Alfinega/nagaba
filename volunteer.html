Join Our Association

NAGABA welcomes anyone who shares our vision of empowering rural women, youth, and the needy. Membership is open to individuals and organisations in sympathy with our aims.

We have four membership categories — Golden Youth, Full Members, Associate Members, and Honorary Members — each with distinct rights and benefits outlined in our constitution.

Apply for Membership Membership Categories

Membership Categories

Golden Youth

The original founding promoters of NAGABA. They hold exclusive rights including sitting on the executive committee and decisions on winding up the organisation.

Full Members

Individuals who participate in all activities, pay membership fees, and have the privilege of voting in elections and serving on committees.

Associate Members

Community members and institutions interested in working with NAGABA. They participate in meetings and benefit from services but do not vote.

Honorary Members

Distinguished individuals who have contributed exceptional service. Membership is by invitation and is exempt from subscription fees.

How to Join NAGABA

Qualification for Membership

A person qualifies for full membership if they:

  • Are a resident in Uganda or hold a recognised work permit
  • Are contributing to community development
  • Are above eight years of age
  • Subscribe to the objectives of the organisation
  • Pay membership and subscription fees as prescribed
  • Register with the organisation’s Secretary

Duties of Members

Every member of NAGABA shall:

  • Comply with the constitution and resolutions of general meetings
  • Promote, develop, and protect the interests of the organisation
  • Meet all financial obligations as determined
  • Do work assigned by the management committee or general meeting
  • Promote discipline and good relationships
  • Ensure safety of all organisation properties

HIV/AIDS Outreach

Join our awareness campaigns and help educate communities on prevention, testing, and care support.

Skills Training

Volunteer as a trainer in tailoring, knitting, crafts, or business planning to help women build livelihoods.

Agriculture Projects

Support our farming initiatives including bee keeping, fish farming, cattle rearing, poultry, and goat rearing projects.
