Education June 15, 2024

New Community Center Opens

We are thrilled to announce the grand opening of our new community center in the heart of the city. This space will host after-school programs, health clinics, and community events. Over 1,000 families will benefit from the resources and support offered here.

The center features modern classrooms, a health clinic, computer lab, and multipurpose spaces for community gatherings. Local businesses have partnered with us to provide job training and placement services.
