Our Programmes

NAGABA is developing community-driven programmes that address the social and economic needs of rural women, youth, and the needy in Ntungamo District.

Each planned programme is rooted in our constitution's objectives — from HIV/AIDS awareness to income-generating projects — designed to build self-reliance and transform livelihoods in the future.

Our Core Programmes

HIV/AIDS Awareness & Care

We plan to raise HIV/AIDS awareness within our communities, providing education, support, and care referrals for those infected and affected. We aim to build capacity among NGOs, CBOs, FBOs, and government bodies to better support women and children living with HIV/AIDS.

Learn More

Skills Training & Empowerment

We aim to empower women and youth through hands-on training in tailoring, knitting, craft-making, small-scale business planning, and credit and finance management — building the foundation for economic independence.

Learn More

Agriculture & Livelihoods

We plan to create income-generating opportunities through bee keeping, fish farming, fruit growing, cash crops, cattle rearing, poultry, and goat projects — applying improved modern and appropriate agro-technologies.

HIV/AIDS Awareness & Care

We aim to raise awareness and build community capacity to support those affected by HIV/AIDS.

  • Community Education: Outreach sessions on HIV/AIDS prevention, testing, and treatment
  • Support & Care: Connecting infected and affected individuals with healthcare resources
  • Capacity Building: Training other NGOs, CBOs, and FBOs to better serve their communities
  • Advocacy: Advocating for the rights of women and children in relation to HIV/AIDS
Get Involved

Skills Training & Empowerment

We aim to equip women and youth with practical skills for economic independence.

  • Tailoring & Knitting: Hands-on training in garment making and textile crafts
  • Handcrafts: Creating marketable products from local materials
  • Business Planning: Small-scale business startup and management skills
  • Financial Literacy: Credit and finance management for sustainable livelihoods
Support Training

Agriculture & Livelihoods

We plan to create sustainable income-generating opportunities through improved agricultural practices.

  • Bee Keeping: Honey production for household income and nutrition
  • Fish Farming: Aquaculture projects for food security and market sale
  • Livestock: Cattle rearing, poultry, and goat rearing projects for rural families
  • Crop Farming: Fruit growing and cash crops using modern agro-technologies
