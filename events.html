Our Planned Activities

As a newly established community-based organisation, NAGABA is preparing a range of activities for the future. These are the initiatives we aim to roll out as our programmes launch.

HIV/AIDS Awareness Outreach

Community outreach sessions planned to educate families on HIV/AIDS prevention, testing, and support services available in the region.

Status: In planning

Skills Training Workshops

Hands-on training sessions in tailoring, knitting, and craft-making for women and youth — building practical skills for economic independence.

Status: In planning

Agricultural Field Days

Training days on improved farming — bee keeping, fish farming, cattle rearing, poultry, and goat projects using modern agro-technologies.

Status: In planning

Women's Rights Advocacy

Advocacy campaigns for the rights of women and children — engaging local authorities and building a collective voice in district planning.

Status: In planning

Financial Literacy Training

Sessions on small-scale business planning, credit and finance management — empowering members to start and sustain their own enterprises.

Status: In planning

Annual General Meeting

Our constitution requires an AGM within the first three months of each year — where members receive reports, elect leadership, and plan the way forward.

Status: Annual

How Your Support Enables Our Work

None of these activities can happen without resources. Here is how donor support will translate directly into community impact.

Equipment & Materials

Sewing machines, knitting needles, fabric, beehives, fishing nets, and livestock — the tools our members need to turn skills into livelihoods.

Training Costs

Facilitator fees, venue hire, training manuals, and refreshments for skills workshops and awareness outreaches across our target communities.

Community Outreach

Transport, printed materials, and volunteer support to carry HIV/AIDS awareness and women's rights advocacy into rural communities.

Operational Foundation

Establishing an office, registering bank accounts, and building the administrative foundation that enables sustained programme delivery.
